# Formula High Ticket (FHT) Methodology

When asked to structure a High Ticket (HT) product or write a landing page for one, strictly follow these structural guidelines. Do not present a simple list of features. Instead, present a **Journey** where each deliverable has a specific function and removes a specific objection.

## The 4 Pillars of a High Ticket

1. **Direcionamento (Estratégico)**
   - **Diagnóstico Individual:** 1:1 session to map the client's scenario, define the "Roma" (transformation), and build a customized action plan. Removes the objection: "I don't know where to start."
   - **SNA (Sistema de Navegação Ativa):** A step-by-step checklist tracker (often a Google Sheet) for the client.
   - **Navegador:** A dedicated team member who follows up with the client to ensure engagement.

2. **Organização (Implementação)**
   - **Drive HT:** A centralized Google Drive with all templates, checklists, and documents ready to use. Removes the objection: "I won't be able to replicate this alone."
   - **Implementation Days (Bootcamps):** Intensive live group meetings focused purely on execution (Ensino, Exemplo, Exercício, Exame). Removes the objection: "I'll get content but won't be able to apply it."

3. **Feedback**
   - **Plantão de Dúvidas:** Group Q&A sessions.
   - **Consultoria Individual:** 1:1 sessions for specific roadblocks.
   - **Análise de Lançamento/Projeto:** Deep-dive reviews of the client's execution.

4. **Ambiente / Conexão**
   - **Comunidade Virtual:** Telegram/WhatsApp group for networking and insights.
   - **Encontros Exclusivos:** Live or offline events for total immersion.
   - **Área de Parcerias:** Stimulating business between members.

## Landing Page Copy Structure

When writing a landing page or offer structure, use this exact sequence:

1. **A Jornada (The Journey)**
   Explain the sequence: Diagnóstico → Plano de Ação → Implementação → Acompanhamento.
2. **Entregáveis Condicionais (The Deliverables)**
   For each deliverable, state:
   - What it is
   - How it works
   - What the participant receives
   - **Crucial:** What problem it solves and what objection it removes.
3. **Onboarding**
   Clearly define what happens immediately after purchase: Questionário, Agendamento, Acesso ao Kit, Início do Bootcamp.
4. **Logística (Placeholders)**
   Never invent dates, meeting counts, or formats if the user hasn't provided them. Use placeholders like `[definir]`, `[definir duração]`, or `[definir quórum mínimo]`.
